# ICAR-CICR & Karnataka Package of Practices: Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum / arboreum)

## 1. Overview & Agro-Climatic Requirements
- **Institution**: ICAR - Central Institute for Cotton Research (CICR) & UAS Dharwad (Agricultural Research Station Dharwad/Siruguppa).
- **Key Karnataka Hybrids & Varieties**: DCH-32, Bunny Bt, RCH-2 Bt, Mallika Bt, Sahana, Jayadhar (desi cotton for rainfed northern black soils).
- **Soil & pH**: Deep black cotton soils (vertisols) or fertile sandy loams with excellent drainage. pH 6.5 to 8.5. Tolerates mild salinity.
- **Climate & Temperature**: Warm climate with abundant sunshine. Minimum temperature for germination 16°C; optimal vegetative growth 21°C - 30°C. Water requirement 700-1200 mm.
- **Fertilizer Dose**: Irrigated Bt cotton: 150:75:75 kg N:P2O5:K2O per ha. Split nitrogen: 25% basal, 25% square initiation (35 DAS), 25% flowering (60 DAS), 25% boll development (80 DAS). Spray 1% Magnesium Sulphate + 1% 19:19:19 to prevent leaf reddening.

## 2. Major Cotton Diseases & Integrated Management

### A. Bacterial Blight / Black Arm (Xanthomonas citri pv. malvacearum)
- **Symptoms**:
  - Angular leaf spot: Small, angular, dark green water-soaked spots delimited by leaf veinlets.
  - Black arm: Black elongated cankers on petioles and stems, causing branch girdling and breakage.
  - Boll rot: Round, water-soaked black spots on bolls leading to internal lint discoloration.
- **Favorable Weather**: High humidity (>85%), temperatures 28°C - 35°C, windy rainstorms.
- **Management**:
  - Delint acid-delinted seeds with concentrated H2SO4 (100 ml/kg seed) followed by seed treatment with Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/kg.
  - Spray Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L mixed with Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/L (1 g in 10 L water) at 15-day intervals upon first appearance of angular spots.

### B. Alternaria Leaf Spot (Alternaria macrospora)
- **Symptoms**: Brown, circular spots with concentric rings on leaves. As spots coalesce, center dries, cracks, and falls out creating a "shot-hole" effect. Severe infection triggers heavy premature defoliation.
- **Management**:
  - Balanced nutrition: Avoid potassium deficiency which predisposes cotton to Alternaria.
  - Foliar spray: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1.0 ml/L or Kresoxim-methyl 44.3% SC @ 1.0 ml/L.

### C. Cotton Sucking Pest Complex (Thrips, Jassids, Aphids, Whiteflies)
- **Symptoms**:
  - Jassids / Leafhoppers (Amrasca biguttula): Yellowing and downward curling of leaf margins ("hopper burn").
  - Thrips: Silvery patches on lower leaf surface, curling upward of leaf margins.
  - Whiteflies (Bemisia tabaci): Chlorosis, honeydew excretion causing sooty mold, vector for Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV).
- **Integrated Management**:
  - Seed treatment with Imidacloprid 70% WS @ 5 g/kg seed.
  - Install yellow and blue sticky traps @ 15-20 traps/acre.
  - Encourage natural predators: Ladybird beetles, Chrysoperla.
  - Chemical spray (Economic Threshold Level ETL reached): Flonicamid 50% WG @ 0.3 g/L or Diafenthiuron 50% WP @ 1.0 g/L or Dinotefuran 20% SG @ 0.4 g/L.

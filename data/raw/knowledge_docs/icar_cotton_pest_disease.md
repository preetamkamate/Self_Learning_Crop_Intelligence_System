# ICAR-CICR & UAS Dharwad Package of Practices: Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum) Pest & Disease Management

## 1. Overview & Crop Ecology
- **Institutions**: ICAR - Central Institute for Cotton Research (CICR), Nagpur & UAS Dharwad (Agricultural Research Station, Dharwad).
- **Major Cotton Tracts in Karnataka**: Dharwad, Belagavi, Haveri, Ballari, Raichur, Kalaburagi.
- **Optimum Soil**: Deep black cotton soils (Vertisols) with good moisture retention and pH 6.5 - 8.0.

## 2. Major Diseases, Symptoms & Integrated Management

### A. Bacterial Blight / Angular Leaf Spot / Blackarm (Xanthomonas citri pv. malvacearum)
- **Primary Source**: ICAR-CICR Disease Identification and Control Manual.
- **Symptoms**:
  - Angular, water-soaked translucent lesions bounded by leaf veins on foliage. Lesions turn reddish-brown to dark black.
  - Blackarm phase: Deep black elongated lesions on stems and branches causing girdling and breaking of fruiting branches.
  - Boll rot: Dark, sunken circular spots on developing bolls rotting internal lint.
- **Management Strategy**:
  - Acid delinting of cotton seeds with commercial concentrated H2SO4 @ 100 ml/kg seed.
  - Spray Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/L (1 g/10 L) combined with Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L. Repeat at 15-day intervals if rainfall continues.

### B. Alternaria Leaf Spot (Alternaria macrospora)
- **Symptoms**: Concentric circular to irregular brown spots with purple margins on leaves, primarily during boll development stage under humid cloudy weather.
- **Management**:
  - Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1.0 ml/L or Pyraclostrobin 20% WG @ 1.0 g/L or Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L.

### C. Grey Mildew / Dahiya Disease (Ramularia areola)
- **Symptoms**: Angular pale translucent spots on the lower leaf surface covered with a powdery frost-white fungal growth. Leaves turn yellow, dry out, and shed prematurely.
- **Management**:
  - Foliar spray of Wettable Sulfur 80% WDG @ 3.0 g/L or Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1.0 g/L or Kresoxim-methyl 44.3% SC @ 1.0 ml/L.

### D. Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella)
- **Symptoms**: Rosetted flowers (petals twisted into rosette), premature boll opening (locule damage), double seeds inside bolls, and staining of lint.
- **Management**:
  - Install delta/sleeve pheromone traps with Gossyplure @ 5 traps/acre for monitoring (economic threshold: 8 moths/trap/night for 3 consecutive days).
  - Release *Trichogramma bactrae* egg parasitoids @ 60,000/acre at weekly intervals.
  - For chemical knockdown: Spray Profenofos 50% EC @ 2.0 ml/L or Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 ml/L.

# ICAR-IIHR & UAS Dharwad Package of Practices: Chilli (Capsicum annuum) Integrated Pest & Disease Management

## 1. Agro-Climatic Requirements & Overview
- **Institutions**: ICAR - Indian Institute of Horticultural Research (IIHR), Bengaluru & University of Agricultural Sciences (UAS), Dharwad.
- **Recommended Commercial Cultivars**: Arka Meghana, Arka Harita, Arka Suphal, Byadagi Kaddi, Byadagi Dabbi (Karnataka GI Tag).
- **Soil & Climate**: Well-drained black soil or rich sandy loam. Optimum soil pH 6.2 to 7.0. Optimum temperature 20°C to 30°C. Heavy rainfall during flowering causes severe flower drop and anthracnose fruit rot.

## 2. Major Diseases & Sourced Management Guidelines

### A. Chilli Anthracnose / Fruit Rot / Die-Back (Colletotrichum capsici)
- **Primary Source**: ICAR-IIHR Technical Bulletin on Solanaceous Vegetable Protection.
- **Symptoms**:
  - Foliar Die-back: Drying of twigs from tip downward, twigs turn white or greyish with black dot-like acervuli.
  - Fruit Rot: Circular, sunken, water-soaked dark lesions on ripe red fruits with concentric rings of salmon-pink/black fruiting bodies.
- **Cultural & Sanitation**:
  - Seed treatment with *Trichoderma harzianum* or *T. viride* @ 10 g/kg seed.
  - Collect and burn infected twigs and mummified fruits. Avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation during fruiting.
- **Organic & Biological Control**:
  - Spray *Pseudomonas fluorescens* (IIHR formulation) @ 5 g/L or cold-pressed Neem Oil (10,000 ppm) @ 3 ml/L at flowering onset.
- **Chemical Control (CIBRC Approved Dosages)**:
  - Preventive: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L.
  - Curative / High Infection: Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1.0 ml/L, or Tebuconazole 50% + Trifloxystrobin 25% WG (Nativo) @ 0.6 g/L, or Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 0.5 ml/L.
  - Spray volume: 500 L/ha. Wait period (PHI): 5-7 days before harvest.

### B. Chilli Leaf Curl Disease / Murda Complex (Thrips & Mites Vector Complex)
- **Primary Source**: UAS Dharwad Raitha Mitra Extension Guide.
- **Symptoms**:
  - Upward curling of leaf margins with elongation into boat-like shape caused by Scirtothrips dorsalis (Chilli Thrips).
  - Downward curling of leaf margins with inverted boat shape, thickened leathery leaves caused by Polyphagotarsonemus latus (Yellow Mite).
  - Severe stunting, rosette appearance, flower drop, and malformed small fruits.
- **Vector Integrated Management**:
  - Install blue sticky traps @ 25 traps/acre for thrips and yellow sticky traps for whiteflies.
  - Intercrop with 2-3 border rows of maize or sorghum to impede insect vector drift.
  - For Thrips: Spray Fipronil 5% SC @ 1.5 ml/L or Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 0.9 ml/L.
  - For Yellow Mites: Spray Spiromesifen 22.9% SC @ 1.0 ml/L or Diafenthiuron 50% WP @ 1.2 g/L. Alternate with Neem seed extract 5%.

### C. Bacterial Leaf Spot (Xanthomonas campestris pv. vesicatoria)
- **Symptoms**: Small, dark brown water-soaked circular lesions on leaves, often surrounded by yellow halos. Lesions coalesce causing severe blighting and premature defoliation.
- **Management**:
  - Spray Streptocycline (90:10 Streptomycin + Tetracycline) @ 0.1 g/L (1 g in 10 L water) mixed with Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L at 10-12 day intervals.

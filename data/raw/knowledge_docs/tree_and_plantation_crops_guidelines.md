## 2. Coconut Palm (Cocos nucifera) Multi-Part Diseases

### A. Coconut Stem Bleeding (Thielaviopsis paradoxa / Ceratocystis paradoxa)
- **Primary Source**: ICAR-CPCRI Technical Bulletin No. 58 (Coconut Disease Management).
- **Affected Plant Part**: Trunk, Bark, Stem lower column.
- **Symptoms**:
  - Dark brown or reddish-brown liquid exudes from longitudinal cracks on the stem bark, typically within 2-3 meters from ground level.
  - The exuded sap dries into a dark, viscous, tarry patch. Internal stem tissue rots into a fibrous brown mass.
- **Integrated Management**:
  - Chisel out affected decayed fibrous bark and wood tissue cleanly until healthy tissue is reached.
  - Swab the wound with Coal Tar or Bordeaux Paste (1 kg Copper Sulfate + 1 kg Slaked Lime in 10 L water).
  - Apply 5 kg neem cake per palm around the basin.
  - Root feeding: In severe cases, root-feed with Hexaconazole 5% SC @ 2 ml in 100 ml water per palm.

### B. Coconut Bud Rot (Phytophthora palmivora)
- **Affected Plant Part**: Crown, spindle leaf, apical bud.
- **Symptoms**: Yellowing and withered drooping of the central spindle leaf. The central spear leaf rots easily when pulled, emitting a foul odor.
- **Management**:
  - Cut away rotted heart tissues cleanly. Place 2 sachets of Mancozeb 75% WP (5 g each wrapped in perforated cloth) into the leaf axils of the crown.
  - Prophylactic spraying: Spray Bordeaux Mixture 1% on the crown before the onset of monsoon rains.

## 3. Citrus (Citrus spp.) Multi-Part Diseases

### A. Citrus Canker (Xanthomonas citri subsp. citri)
- **Primary Source**: ICAR-CCRI Disease Advisory Bulletin.
- **Affected Plant Part**: Leaf, Twig, Stem, Fruit peel.
- **Symptoms**:
  - Leaves: Raised, blister-like corky lesions with crater-like centers, surrounded by a distinct oily or yellow halo.
  - Stems/Twigs: Rough, brown corky eruptions causing twig dieback.
  - Fruits: Dark, rough, cratered scabby eruptions on the rind. Internal fruit flesh is unaffected but unmarketable.
- **Management**:
  - Prune and burn infected twigs before monsoon.
  - Spray Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/L (1 g/10 L) mixed with Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L at 20-day intervals during new vegetative flushes.

### B. Citrus Greening / Huanglongbing (Candidatus Liberibacter asiaticus)
- **Affected Plant Part**: Whole Canopy, Foliage, Fruit.
- **Symptoms**: Asymmetric blotchy mottle chlorosis across leaf veins (veins do not remain neatly green as in iron deficiency). Upright, small leaves. Fruits remain small, lopsided, poorly colored with bitter juice.
- **Vector Control**: Diaphorina citri (Asian Citrus Psyllid). Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.5 ml/L or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.3 g/L during active leaf flush.

# ICAR-NRRI & UAS Bengaluru Package of Practices: Paddy Rice (Oryza sativa) Disease & Crop Protection

## 1. Overview & Agro-Ecological Zones
- **Institutions**: ICAR - National Rice Research Institute (NRRI), Cuttack & University of Agricultural Sciences, Bengaluru / Mandya ZARS (V.C. Farm).
- **Major Karnataka Rice Ecosystems**: Cauvery Irrigation Basin (Mandya, Mysuru), Tunga-Bhadra Basin (Shivamogga, Ballari), Coastal Rainfed Lowlands (Udupi, Dakshina Kannada).
- **Varieties**: Jyothi, Jaya, IR-64, BPT-5204 (Samba Mahsuri), RNR-15048 (Telangana Sona / Low GI).

## 2. Major Diseases & Integrated Control

### A. Rice Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae / Pyricularia oryzae)
- **Primary Source**: ICAR-NRRI Plant Pathology Compendium.
- **Symptoms**:
  - Leaf Blast: Spindle-shaped or eye-shaped lesions with grey/whitish centers and dark brown or reddish-brown borders. Spots coalesce causing extensive leaf drying (burn).
  - Neck Blast: Dark brown to black rot at the base of the panicle neck. Panicles break over, grains remain chaffy / empty (severest yield loss).
- **Cultural Practices**:
  - Avoid excessive nitrogenous fertilizer application (>120 kg N/ha in single splits). Split N in 3 doses (basal, tillering, panicle initiation).
- **Chemical Control (CIBRC Approved)**:
  - Prophylactic / Nursery: Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L or Isoprothiolane 40% EC @ 1.5 ml/L.
  - Curative at Neck Blast stage: Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 1.0 ml/L or Trifloxystrobin 25% + Tebuconazole 50% WG @ 0.4 g/L.

### B. Bacterial Leaf Blight (Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae)
- **Symptoms**: Water-soaked stripes beginning at leaf tips and margins, expanding downward with characteristic wavy margins. Lesions turn straw-yellow to dirty white. In mornings, golden-yellow bacterial beads ooze from leaf margins.
- **Management**:
  - Drain standing water from the field for 2-3 days to lower canopy humidity.
  - Avoid top dressing nitrogen during disease flare-up.
  - Spray Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L mixed with Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/L (1 g/10 L).

### C. Sheath Blight (Rhizoctonia solani)
- **Symptoms**: Oval or snake-skin like greenish-grey lesions with distinct brown irregular margins appearing on leaf sheaths near water level. Under high humidity, lesions spread upward into upper leaves and flag leaves.
- **Management**:
  - Spray Validamycin 3% L @ 2.0 ml/L or Hexaconazole 5% SC @ 2.0 ml/L or Thifluzamide 24% SC @ 0.75 ml/L at boot-leaf stage.

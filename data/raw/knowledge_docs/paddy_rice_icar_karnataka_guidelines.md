# ICAR-NRRI & Karnataka Raitha Mitra Package of Practices: Paddy / Rice (Oryza sativa)

## 1. Overview & Agro-Climatic Requirements
- **Institution**: ICAR - National Rice Research Institute (NRRI) & University of Agricultural Sciences (UAS) Bengaluru & Dharwad.
- **Key Karnataka Varieties**:
  - Irrigated/Wetland: Jaya, Jyothi, MTU-1010, BPT-5204 (Samba Mahsuri), IR-64, KMP-101 (Thanu), BR-2655.
  - Rainfed/Upland: Dhiren, Sahbhagi Dhan, Intan.
- **Soil & pH**: Clayey to clay loam soils with high water holding capacity. Optimal pH: 5.5 to 7.0. Can tolerate moderate salinity up to EC 4.0 dS/m.
- **Climate & Rainfall**: Warm, humid climate. Optimum temperature 22°C to 32°C. Annual water requirement 1100-1400 mm.
- **Fertilizer Recommendation (NPK for Karnataka)**:
  - High yielding varieties: 100:50:50 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare.
  - Split application of Nitrogen: 50% at basal, 25% at tillering (20-25 DAT), 25% at panicle initiation. Apply Zinc Sulphate @ 25 kg/ha basal.

## 2. Major Paddy Diseases & Management

### A. Rice Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae / Pyricularia oryzae)
- **Symptoms**:
  - Leaf Blast: Spindle-shaped or eye-shaped spots with grey or whitish centers and brown/reddish margins. Spots enlarge and coalesce, causing entire leaves to wither and dry.
  - Neck Blast: Brown to black lesion encircling the panicle base/neck node; panicles break and hang down, producing chaffy unfilled grains.
- **Favorable Weather**: Relative humidity >90%, dew formation, night temperatures between 18°C - 24°C, excess nitrogenous fertilization.
- **Cultural Practices**:
  - Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilizer. Split nitrogen into 3-4 doses.
  - Treat seeds before sowing with Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 2 g/kg or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10 g/kg seed.
- **Chemical Control**:
  - Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP (Beam) @ 0.6 g/L water, or Isoprothiolane 40% EC @ 1.5 ml/L, or Kasugamycin 3% SL @ 2.5 ml/L.
  - In severe neck blast risk areas, apply preventive spray at heading stage.

### B. Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB - Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae)
- **Symptoms**: Water-soaked stripes starting from leaf tips and margins, expanding into wavy, yellowish-white lesions running parallel to veins. Tiny amber droplets of bacterial ooze appear on young lesions in early morning. "Kresek" (seedling wilt) stage causes complete seedling drying.
- **Favorable Weather**: Heavy rainfall, strong winds during monsoon, high humidity, water stagnation.
- **Management**:
  - Drain excess field water temporarily.
  - Avoid top dressing nitrogen during disease flare-up; apply additional Muriate of Potash (MOP) @ 25 kg/ha to improve plant immunity.
  - Spray Copper Hydroxide 53.8% DF @ 2.0 g/L or Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L mixed with Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/L (1 g in 10 L water).

### C. Rice Sheath Blight (Rhizoctonia solani)
- **Symptoms**: Oval or irregular greenish-grey water-soaked spots on leaf sheaths near water line. Spots enlarge with dark brown boundaries and greyish white center ("snake skin" pattern). Lesions spread up to flag leaf.
- **Management**:
  - Deep summer ploughing and destruction of infected stubble.
  - Foliar spray of Hexaconazole 5% SC @ 2.0 ml/L or Validamycin 3% L @ 2.0 ml/L or Propiconazole 25% EC (Tilt) @ 1.0 ml/L.

### D. Brown Plant Hopper (BPH - Nilaparvata lugens)
- **Symptoms**: Both nymphs and adults suck sap from leaf sheath bases. Plants turn yellow, dry up rapidly and lodge in circular patches known as "Hopper Burn".
- **Management**:
  - Provide "Alley ways" (skip one row after every 8-10 rows) for light penetration and air movement.
  - Alternate wetting and drying (AWD) water management.
  - Spray Pymetrozine 50% WG @ 0.6 g/L or Triflumezopyrim 10% SC @ 0.48 ml/L or Dinotefuran 20% SG @ 0.4 g/L directed at stem bases.

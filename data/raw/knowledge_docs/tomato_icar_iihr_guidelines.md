# ICAR-IIHR Package of Practices: Tomato (Solanum lycopersicum) Cultivation & Integrated Pest/Disease Management

## 1. Overview & Agro-Climatic Requirements
- **Institution**: ICAR - Indian Institute of Horticultural Research (IIHR), Bengaluru & UAS Bengaluru / Raitha Mitra.
- **Recommended Varieties & Hybrids**: Arka Rakshak (triple disease resistant to ToLCV, BW, Early Blight), Arka Samrat, Arka Vikas, Arka Abhed.
- **Optimal Soil**: Well-drained sandy loam or clay loam, rich in organic matter.
- **Soil pH**: 6.0 to 7.0. EC < 1.0 dS/m.
- **Temperature & Climate**: 21°C to 28°C during vegetative stage; daytime 24°C-30°C, nighttime 16°C-20°C for fruit set. Extreme heat (>35°C) causes flower drop.
- **Water Management**: Drip irrigation recommended; water requirement 400-600 mm throughout crop lifecycle. Avoid wetting foliage during flowering/fruiting to prevent fungal foliar blights.

## 2. Major Diseases, Symptoms & Integrated Management

### A. Tomato Early Blight (Alternaria solani)
- **Symptoms**: Concentric brown-to-black ring spots ("target board" pattern) appearing first on older lower leaves. Leaves turn yellow around spots, dry out, and drop prematurely (defoliation).
- **Favorable Weather**: Warm temperatures (24°C - 30°C) with intermittent rain and high relative humidity (>80%).
- **Cultural & Preventive**:
  - Maintain 60 cm x 45 cm or 90 cm x 60 cm spacing for aeration.
  - Stake plants with bamboo poles to prevent leaves touching wet soil.
  - Remove and burn infected lower leaves up to 30 cm from ground.
- **Organic & Biological Control**:
  - Spray *Pseudomonas fluorescens* or *Bacillus subtilis* @ 5 g/L or 10 ml/L at transplanting and 30 DAT.
  - Foliar spray of 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or Cold-pressed Neem Oil (10,000 ppm) @ 3 ml/L.
- **Chemical Control (CIBRC Approved Dosages)**:
  - Preventive / Initial symptoms: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.0 - 2.5 g/L or Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2.0 g/L.
  - Curative / Severe infection: Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 1.0 ml/L, or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1.0 ml/L.
  - Spray volume: 500 L/ha. Wait period (PHI): 5-7 days before harvest.

### B. Tomato Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)
- **Symptoms**: Water-soaked dark green/black lesions on leaf margins and tips. Under moist morning conditions, white downy fungal mildew appears on leaf undersides. Stems show purplish-brown lesions; fruits develop large, greasy brown firm lesions.
- **Favorable Weather**: Cool temperatures (13°C - 21°C) with prolonged cloud cover, fog, and relative humidity >90%.
- **Cultural Practices**:
  - Avoid night irrigation and overhead sprinklers. Ensure ridge planting with proper soil drainage.
- **Chemical Control**:
  - Prophylactic: Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L.
  - Systemic curative: Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Ridomil Gold) @ 2.5 g/L, or Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.0 g/L, or Dimethomorph 50% WP @ 1.0 g/L.

### C. Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV) & Whitefly Vector
- **Symptoms**: Severe stunting, upward curling and cupping of leaflets, marginal chlorosis (yellowing), puckering, and bushy appearance. Flowers fail to develop; severe yield loss if infected before flowering.
- **Vector**: Bemisia tabaci (Silverleaf Whitefly).
- **Management Strategy**:
  - Grow resistant hybrids like Arka Rakshak or Arka Abhed.
  - Use 40-mesh nylon insect-proof net in nursery beds for first 25-30 days.
  - Install yellow sticky traps @ 20-25 traps per acre at 1 foot above canopy level.
  - Vector control: Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.5 ml/L or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.3 g/L or Acetamiprid 20% SP @ 0.4 g/L. Alternate with neem oil 3 ml/L to prevent pesticide resistance.

### D. Bacterial Wilt (Ralstonia solanacearum)
- **Symptoms**: Rapid daytime wilting of plant while foliage retains green color (green wilt). Stem cross-section placed in water emits white bacterial ooze streams.
- **Management**:
  - Soil solarization in nursery. Apply bleaching powder @ 10 kg/acre before planting.
  - Crop rotation with non-solanaceous crops (maize, ragi, pulses) for 2-3 years.
  - Drench root zone with Copper Hydroxide 77% WP @ 2.0 g/L + Streptocycline (Streptomycin sulphate + Tetracycline) @ 0.1 g/L (1 g in 10 L water).

### E. Tomato Fruit Borer (Helicoverpa armigera)
- **Symptoms**: Circular bore holes on green and ripe fruits, often with caterpillar half inside fruit.
- **Management**:
  - Plant African Marigold as trap crop (1 row of marigold for every 16 rows of tomato).
  - Install Helilure pheromone traps @ 5-8 traps/acre.
  - Spray HaNPV @ 250 LE/ha or Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) @ 2 g/L.
  - Chemical spray: Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC (Coragen) @ 0.3 ml/L or Flubendiamide 39.35% SC @ 0.3 ml/L. PHI: 3 days.

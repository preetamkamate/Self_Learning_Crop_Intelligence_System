# ICAR-IIHR Advanced Advisory: Tomato Multi-Part Disorders, Nutrient Stresses & Viral Vectors

## 1. Overview & Institution Source
- **Primary Source**: ICAR - Indian Institute of Horticultural Research (IIHR), Hesaraghatta, Bengaluru.
- **Reference**: IIHR Horticultural Extension Bulletin No. 42 (Tomato Disease & Abiotic Health Diagnostics).

## 2. Multi-Part Disorders & Abiotic Stresses

### A. Blossom End Rot (Calcium Deficiency & Water Stress)
- **Affected Plant Part**: Fruit (calyx end).
- **Symptoms**: Small, water-soaked, light brownish lesion on the blossom end (distal tip) of green fruit. The spot enlarges rapidly, flattens, and turns dark brown to black, leathery, and sunken.
- **Agronomic Cause**: Inadequate calcium transport during rapid cell division in young fruit, typically triggered by irregular soil moisture fluctuations or high electrical conductivity (EC).
- **Distinction from Anthracnose**: Blossom End Rot is strictly confined to the fruit apex/blossom scar, whereas Anthracnose produces concentric ring lesions across any shoulder surface of ripe fruit.
- **Management**:
  - Maintain regular, uniform drip irrigation; mulch beds to reduce soil moisture evaporation.
  - Foliar spray of Calcium Nitrate @ 4.0 - 5.0 g/L or Chelated Calcium (Ca-EDTA 10%) @ 1.0 g/L every 10 days starting at early fruit set.

### B. Tomato Leaf Curl New Delhi Virus (ToLCNDV) & Whitefly Vector
- **Affected Plant Part**: Canopy, young leaves, flower buds.
- **Symptoms**: Downward and upward curling of leaf margins, thick puckering, vein clearing, severe reduction in leaflet size (stunting), and bushy habit. Complete flower abortion if infected early.
- **Integrated Vector Management**:
  - Nursery protection under 40-mesh insect-proof netting for 25-30 days before field transplanting.
  - Erect 20-25 yellow sticky traps/acre at canopy level.
  - Foliar spray of Diafenthiuron 50% WP @ 1.2 g/L or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.3 g/L or Spiromesifen 22.9% SC @ 1.0 ml/L.

### C. Tomato Pinworm / Leaf Miner (Tuta absoluta)
- **Affected Plant Part**: Foliage, stems, green fruit.
- **Symptoms**: Transparent blister-like blotch mines in leaf parenchyma; pinhole entry punctures around fruit calyx leading to secondary rot.
- **Management**:
  - Install Tuta-specific pheromone lure traps @ 16-20 traps/acre.
  - Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 ml/L or Flubendiamide 39.35% SC @ 0.3 ml/L or Spinosad 45% SC @ 0.3 ml/L.

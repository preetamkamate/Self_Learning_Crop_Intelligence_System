## 2. Soil pH Classification & Nutrient Availability Lockout

### A. Soil pH Spectrum in Karnataka Agricultural Zones
- **Acidic Soils (pH < 5.5)**: Prevalent in Western Ghats, Coastal Zone (Uttara Kannada, Udupi, Dakshina Kannada), and Malnad regions (Shivamogga, Chikkamagaluru, Kodagu).
  - *Nutrient Lockout*: Insoluble fixation of Phosphorus (P) with Aluminum/Iron oxides; Calcium (Ca) and Magnesium (Mg) base deficiency.
  - *Amelioration*: Apply Agricultural Dolomitic Lime @ 200 - 400 kg/acre every 3 years based on buffer pH test.
- **Neutral / Optimum Soils (pH 6.2 - 7.2)**: Southern Dry Zone (Mandya, Mysuru, Hassan, Tumakuru).
  - *Nutrient Bioavailability*: Maximum balanced availability of primary (N, P, K), secondary (S, Ca, Mg), and micronutrients.
- **Alkaline / Calcareous Soils (pH > 7.8, EC > 1.0 dS/m)**: Northern Dry Zone (Vijayapura, Bagalkote, Belagavi, Kalaburagi, Raichur).
  - *Nutrient Lockout*: Free Calcium Carbonate (CaCO3) fixes Phosphorus (P) as insoluble tricalcium phosphate; severe lockout of Iron (Fe), Zinc (Zn), and Manganese (Mn).
  - *Amelioration*: Application of Gypsum @ 500 kg/acre, green manuring with Sunnhemp / Dhaincha (Sesbania), and farmyard manure @ 4 tonnes/acre to generate organic carbonic acid.

## 3. Plant Part Mobility Rules for Foliar Nutrient Diagnostics
- **Mobile Nutrients (N, P, K, Mg)**:
  - Mobilized from mature tissues to younger apical shoots under deficiency.
  - **Diagnostic Rule**: Deficiency symptoms appear **first on older, lower foliage**.
- **Immobile Nutrients (Fe, Zn, S, B, Ca, Cu, Mn)**:
  - Fixed inside cell walls and vascular lignin; cannot be translocated.
  - **Diagnostic Rule**: Deficiency symptoms appear **first on young, newly emerging leaves and terminal buds**.

## 4. University Corrective Formulations & Foliar Dosages

### A. Iron (Fe) Chlorosis Correction (Northern Calcareous Soils)
- **Foliar Spray**: Ferrous Sulfate (FeSO4) @ 5.0 g/L + Citric Acid @ 1.0 g/L (to prevent ferric precipitation). Spray twice at 10-day intervals.
- **Chelated Option**: Fe-EDTA (12% Fe) @ 1.0 - 1.5 g/L during active flush.

### B. Zinc (Zn) Deficiency / Little Leaf Correction
- **Soil Basal Application**: Zinc Sulfate (ZnSO4 21%) @ 10 kg/acre before planting (valid for 2-3 crop cycles).
- **Foliar Spray**: Zinc Sulfate @ 2.5 g/L neutralized with 1.25 g/L slaked lime, or Zn-EDTA (12% Zn) @ 1.0 g/L.

### C. General Balanced Recovery for Stress-Affected Crops
- **Foliar Spray**: Water-soluble 19:19:19 (NPK) @ 5.0 g/L mixed with multi-micronutrient mixture (Grade-1 Karnataka formulation) @ 2.5 ml/L.

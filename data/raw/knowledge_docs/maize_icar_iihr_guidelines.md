# ICAR-IIMR & Karnataka Package of Practices: Maize / Corn (Zea mays)

## 1. Overview & Agro-Climatic Requirements
- **Institution**: ICAR - Indian Institute of Maize Research (IIMR) & UAS Dharwad / Bengaluru.
- **Key Karnataka Hybrids**: Deccan 101, Deccan 103, Hema (NAH-1137), Nithyashree (NAH-2049), CP-818, Kargil 900M, Pioneer 3396.
- **Soil & pH**: Deep, well-drained fertile loam to silt loam soils rich in organic matter. Sensitive to water stagnation. Optimal pH: 6.0 to 7.5.
- **Climate & Temperature**: Warm-weather crop. Optimum germination temp 20°C - 24°C, growth temp 24°C - 32°C. Water requirement 500-650 mm.
- **Fertilizer Dose (Karnataka standard)**: 150:75:40 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare. 1/3rd N + full P & K as basal; 1/3rd N at knee-high stage (30 DAS); 1/3rd N at tasseling stage.

## 2. Major Maize Diseases & Integrated Management

### A. Common Rust (Puccinia sorghi)
- **Symptoms**: Small, powdery, circular to elongate golden-brown to cinnamon-brown pustules appearing on both upper and lower leaf surfaces. Later in the season, pustules turn brownish-black as teliospores form. Leaves turn chlorotic and desiccate prematurely under heavy infection.
- **Favorable Weather**: Cool temperatures (16°C - 24°C) with high relative humidity (>95%) and persistent morning dew, especially in late Kharif or Rabi plantings.
- **Management**:
  - Plant tolerant hybrids such as Hema or Deccan 103.
  - At first appearance of pustules: Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 1.0 ml/L. Repeat after 15 days if rust pressure persists.

### B. Turcicum Leaf Blight / Northern Leaf Blight (Exserohilum turcicum)
- **Symptoms**: Long, elliptical, spindle-shaped greyish-green to tan lesions (2.5 to 15 cm long) with distinct margins. Under humid conditions, dark grey olivaceous spores cover lesions. Coalescing lesions cause extensive blighting ("fired" look).
- **Favorable Weather**: Moderate temperature (18°C - 27°C) accompanied by heavy dew and overcast rainy skies.
- **Management**:
  - Seed treatment: Metalaxyl + Mancozeb (Apron XL) @ 2 g/kg seed.
  - Foliar spray: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Hexaconazole 5% EC @ 2 ml/L or Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 ml/L at initiation of lower leaf lesions.

### C. Fall Armyworm (FAW - Spodoptera frugiperda)
- **Significance**: Major invasive destructive pest causing severe defoliation and ear damage across Karnataka maize tracts.
- **Symptoms**: Ragged leaf feeding holes, skeletonized whorl leaves filled with sawdust-like frass; central growing whorl chewed off ("dead heart").
- **Integrated Management**:
  - Deep summer ploughing to expose pupae to birds and sunlight.
  - Seed treatment with Cyantraniliprole 19.8% + Thiamethoxam 19.8% FS @ 6 ml/kg seed (protects crop for first 20 days).
  - Install FAW pheromone traps @ 5-6 traps/acre.
  - Early whorl application (15-25 DAS): Spray *Bacillus thuringiensis* (Bt) @ 2 g/L or *Metarhizium anisopliae* @ 5 g/L.
  - Chemical intervention (if >10% whorls infested): Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.4 ml/L or Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 0.5 ml/L directed into leaf whorls.

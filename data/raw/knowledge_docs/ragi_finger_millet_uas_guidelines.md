# UAS Bengaluru Package of Practices: Ragi / Finger Millet (Eleusine coracana)

## 1. Overview & Agro-Climatic Requirements
- **Institution**: University of Agricultural Sciences (UAS), Bengaluru & Mandya/Mysuru Dry Farming Stations.
- **Key Karnataka Varieties**:
  - High Yielding & Blast Resistant: GPU-28, ML-365 (drought tolerant, blast resistant), KMR-301, GPU-48, Indaf-8, Indaf-9, MR-1, MR-6.
- **Soil & Climate**:
  - Red loamy, sandy loam, lateritic soils with good internal drainage.
  - Soil pH: 5.5 to 7.5.
  - Temperature: 22°C to 30°C. Thrives under 600-900 mm rainfall, highly drought hardy with C4 carbon fixation pathway.
- **Sowing Window**:
  - Kharif rainfed: July 1st to July 25th.
  - Late Kharif: up to August 15th (using short duration varieties like GPU-48 or ML-365).
  - Seed rate: Drilling/Broadcasting 10-12 kg/ha; Transplanting 5 kg/ha.

## 2. Major Ragi Diseases & Integrated Management

### A. Ragi Blast (Pyricularia grisea)
- **Significance**: Most destructive disease of finger millet in Karnataka, affecting leaf, neck, and finger stages.
- **Symptoms**:
  - Leaf Blast: Diamond/spindle shaped lesions with grey ash center and reddish-brown borders on seedlings and leaves.
  - Neck Blast: Inverted dark brown to black ring at the junction below earhead panicle; earhead turns premature white and lodges.
  - Finger Blast: Dark brown infection on individual fingers of earhead, inhibiting grain filling and leading to shrivelled grains.
- **Favorable Weather**: Continuous cloudy weather, drizzle, high relative humidity (>85%), temperatures between 20°C - 26°C.
- **Cultural & Preventive**:
  - Cultivate resistant cultivars: GPU-28, ML-365, or KMR-301.
  - Seed Treatment: Treat seeds with Carbendazim 50% WP @ 2.0 g/kg or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10 g/kg seed before sowing.
- **Chemical Control**:
  - At first appearance of leaf blast: Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.0 g/L or Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L.
  - Protect earhead (neck/finger blast): Crucial single spray at 50% earhead emergence with Kitazin (IBP) 48% EC @ 1.0 ml/L or Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L.

### B. Foot Rot / Seedling Blight (Sclerotium rolfsii / Helminthosporium nodulosum)
- **Symptoms**: Brown lesions on basal sheath near soil line, rotting of seedlings, white cottony mycelium at collar region, damping-off patches in nursery.
- **Management**:
  - Seed treatment with *Trichoderma harzianum* @ 5 g/kg seed.
  - Soil solarization and avoid waterlogging. Drench soil patch with Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1 g/L or Copper Oxychloride @ 2.5 g/L.

### C. Major Pests of Ragi
- **Ragi Stem Borer (Sesamia inferens)**: Larvae bore into central shoot causing "dead heart" in vegetative phase and white earhead in flowering stage.
  - Control: Apply Cartap Hydrochloride 4G granules @ 8 kg/acre or spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 ml/L.
- **Ragi Earhead Caterpillar**: Caterpillars feed on milk-stage grain within earheads.
  - Control: Dust Quinalphos 1.5% DP @ 10 kg/acre or spray Novaluron 10% EC @ 1.0 ml/L at grain formation.

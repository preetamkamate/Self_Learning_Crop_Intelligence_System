1. **`plantwild_v2`**:
   - Reference: In-situ real-camera field crop disease collection.
   - License / Terms: Non-commercial academic research use only.
   - Status: Usable real-camera canopy and whole-plant images extracted into `data/manifests/whole_plant_canopy_manifest.json`.

2. **`PlantDoc-Dataset-master`**:
   - Reference: Singh, D., Jain, N., Jain, P., Kayal, P., Kumawat, S., & Batra, N. (2019). *PlantDoc: A Dataset for Visual Plant Disease Detection in Natural Field Conditions*. CoDS-COMAD.
   - License / Terms: Academic research use only.
   - Status: Real in-situ field photos extracted into `data/manifests/whole_plant_canopy_manifest.json`.

3. **`PlantVillage-Dataset-master`**:
   - Reference: Hughes, D., & Salathé, M. (2015). *An open access repository of images on plant health to enable the development of mobile disease diagnostics*. Nature Scientific Data.
   - License / Terms: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0).
   - Status: Synthetic black-background cutouts and grayscale transformations permanently excluded from training. Foliar samples utilized for leaf experimental testing.

4. **`plant-pathology-2021-fgvc8`**:
   - Reference: Fine-Grained Visual Categorization (FGVC8) Workshop / Kaggle Competition.
   - License / Terms: Non-Commercial Research Use Only.
   - Status: Close-up apple foliar leaf images included in leaf experimental testing. Orchard trees have no whole-plant disease labels.

5. **`cassava-leaf-disease-classification`**:
   - Reference: Makerere University AI Lab & FGVC research community.
   - License / Terms: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) / Research Use Only.
   - Status: Foliar cassava leaf images included in leaf experimental testing.

6. **`IP102`**:
   - Reference: Wu, X., Zhan, C., Lai, Y. K., Cheng, M. M., & Yang, J. (2019). *IP102: A Large-Scale Benchmark Dataset for Insect Pest Recognition*. IEEE CVPR.
   - License / Terms: Non-commercial academic research use.
   - Status: Local image directory missing in `~/Downloads` (only `classes.txt` with 102 classes is present); insect pest recognition is safely data-blocked with honest IPM scouting advice.

7. **Agricultural Knowledge & Extension**:
